# Dough Re Mi demo readiness

This is sufficient for a guided prospect demo: four complete routes, real café photography, menu category browsing, location/directions, Instagram, accessible gallery, Light/Dark appearance and clear temporary menu/story copy. It is not an owner-approved production website.

## Ready to demonstrate
- Home, Menu, Our Story and Visit in Light and Dark.
- Real photo gallery with keyboard controls, menu category filtering, directions, and hours guidance linked to Google.
- Two attributed review excerpts, copy address, and share-link fallback.

## Before presenting
- Open Home, Menu, Our Story and Visit; demonstrate a narrow phone viewport and Dark mode.
- Explain that the full menu and business story are awaiting owner input. Category previews are not a priced menu.
- Keep noindex enabled and the Tap & Grow concept credit visible.
- Confirm permission to present the business branding and photographs; repository availability alone does not establish reuse rights.
- Recheck the two attributed Google review excerpts before an owner-approved launch; source and verification date are recorded in DESIGN.md.

## Owner confirmation before production
- Official phone and exact weekly/holiday hours.
- Full menu, prices, availability and any ingredients or dietary claims.
- Approved business story and services, including whether catering, custom cakes or ordering exist.
- Official direct Google review URL for the planned NFC/QR flow; retain the current Google Profile link until supplied.
- Final domain, business contact destination, and permission to use all photos/branding.
- Owner approval of address, Instagram and all public copy.

## Launch work after confirmation
- Replace preview canonical/Open Graph URLs with the official domain.
- Add verified Bakery/CafeOrCoffeeShop structured data; never invent rating, phone, hours, coordinates or menu facts.
- Change indexing only with explicit launch approval.
- Test on real iOS/Android devices and with screen readers; check native browser zoom and native sharing.
- Decide whether to self-host the approved fonts. Review privacy requirements if forms, analytics, booking or other third-party services are later added.

## Not needed for this demo
No new framework, ordering flow, booking system, contact form, analytics, extra sections or fabricated business details are needed. Missing production information can remain gracefully absent. Automated accessibility checks support the demo review but are not certification of full WCAG conformance.
